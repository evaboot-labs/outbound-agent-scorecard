## Who

Heads of Sales Development and SDR managers at software companies in Germany, 51 to 1,000 employees.

## Fit rules

- 5: leads an SDR or BDR team at a software company in the size range.
- 3 to 4: leads sales development, but the company is outside the range or not a pure software company.
- 1 to 2: not a sales development leader, part of a large group that buys centrally, or a competitor (a B2B data provider).

## Offer

Evaboot exports Sales Navigator searches and lists into clean files with verified emails, so SDRs stop building lead lists by hand.

## Proof you may use

None beyond the offer. Never name a customer or a result.

## Ask

A 15-minute call.

## First message rules

- Email when the email is `safe` and its domain is the company's. A LinkedIn connection note otherwise.
- One real fact about the lead or the company, then the offer in one line, then the ask.
- No link, no attachment, no template phrase.
